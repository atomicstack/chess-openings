miss

swiftui color: .purple (#af52de)

you were already winning (engine eval ≥ +200 cp or a mate was on the board) and squandered it with a 300+ cp drop. distinct from blunder — a blunder gives away a balanced position, a miss throws away a won one.

drop of 300+ cp from a non-winning position. game-changing bad.

classification thresholds (in-app)

qualitycentipawn dropextra criteria

best < 5 —

inaccuracy 50 – 99 —

blunder 300+ position wasn't already winning
miss 300+ position WAS already winning (≥ +200 cp or mate)

"drop" = engine's best-move eval minus the eval after your actual move, both from your point of view. mate scores clamp to ±5000 cp.
